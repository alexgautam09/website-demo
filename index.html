LaTeX to AoPS

Paste a proof from your .tex file. The converter swaps claim, proof, remark, motivation, idea, asy and itemize environments for AoPS BBCode as you type, and strips layout commands like \newpage.

Replacement rules

Edit any replacement to change what it becomes. Leave a field empty to just remove the tag. Starred versions (claim*, proof*, remark*, motivation*, idea*) are treated the same as the plain ones. The dashed line is placed above each claim and below its proof's ∎ — if two claims are back to back, the two dashed lines are merged into one so you never get a doubled line. A trailing \\ line break at the end of a line is dropped since AoPS keeps plain line breaks anyway. Everything between asy tags is left exactly as written.
