LaTeX to AoPS

Paste a proof from your .tex file. The converter swaps claim, proof, remark and asy environments for AoPS BBCode as you type, and strips layout commands like \newpage.

Replacement rules

Edit any replacement to change what it becomes. Leave a field empty to just remove the tag. The text after \begin{claim} and \begin{remark} is moved onto the same line as the label. Everything between asy tags is left exactly as written.
